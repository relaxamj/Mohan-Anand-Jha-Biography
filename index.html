About

Mohan Anand Jha is an Indian actor and theatre artist from Darbhanga, Bihar and currently based in Mumbai, Maharashtra. He has appeared in projects including Secret Pocketmaar, Depression, Spooky Love, Savdhaan India: Apni Khaki and the Israeli web series Unconditional (2026). With a strong theatre background, he continues to work across films, television and digital platforms.
