Mohan Anand Jha

Indian Actor

About

Mohan Anand Jha is an Indian actor and theatre artist from Darbhanga, Bihar. He has appeared in projects including Secret Pocketmaar, Depression, Spooky Love, Savdhaan India: Apni Khaki, and the Israeli web series Unconditional (2026). With a strong theatre background, he continues to work across films, television, and digital platforms.
